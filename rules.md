# Reel autopilot rules

The weekly writer reads this file and styles.md before writing the week's scripts, and rewrites only the
"Learned" section at the bottom. Edit anything else by hand whenever you like.

## Who we are

- A web designer who builds fast, phone-friendly websites for US local trade businesses.
- The offer in every reel: a free website check. Comment WEBSITE and we DM you what to fix.
- We have no clients, case studies or results to show yet.

## Audience

- Owners of US local trade businesses: HVAC, roofing, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, garage doors, pest
  control, painting, remodeling, concrete, tree service, pool service, cleaning, auto repair.
- HVAC and roofing come first; rotate the others in.
- They are busy, practical, and watch on their phone between jobs. Talk like a tradesperson, not a marketer.

## The four styles (details and script format in styles.md)

- kinetic, 3 a week: a hot take, myth or question in bold words. Built for comments.
- teardown, 2 a week: we fix a fictional business's site on screen. Built for trust.
- mike, 1 a week: Mike the contractor in a short story, a skit, or myth vs truth. Built for shares.
- ad, 1 a week: the premium before/after commercial. Built for reach.
- Never the same style two days in a row.

## Every reel

- One idea. About 30 seconds. The hook is readable on the first frame and scores 70+ on the hook checker.
- The hook names the trade or a concrete moment ("a leak at 2 AM", "AC out in July").
- Ends on "Comment WEBSITE" and "Send this to a contractor who needs it".
- Caption: first line is the ask, then 2 to 4 short paragraphs, then up to 3 hashtags. The trade is named in
  plain words.

## Never

- Never invent statistics, percentages, studies, clients, results, testimonials or reviews. Scenario details
  ("the page takes 6 seconds") are fine. Claims about the world ("53% of people leave") are not.
- Never name, show or mock a real business or person. Every business shown is fictional and labeled.
- Never mention prices, discounts, deadlines or fake urgency, or promise rankings or a number of jobs.
- Never use em dashes, "game-changer", "unlock", "level up", "in today's video", "stop scrolling", "follow for more".
- Never repeat a topic used in the last 14 days. Rotate through the 20 website problems in styles.md, not just speed.
